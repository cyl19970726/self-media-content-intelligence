# Creator Research Synthesis

Explain the creator from portfolio facts and validated video evidence. Do not fill missing Dashboard sections with generic prose.

## Inputs

- verified profile and collection status;
- reproducible corpus statistics and complete portfolio annotations;
- versioned selection set;
- video reconstructions with CR/DL/VE states;
- bounded public comments and author replies when available;
- publishing dates and public commercial signals when available.

## Required synthesis

- account positioning and one-sentence value proposition;
- audiences served and values provided;
- problems addressed and reasons users may follow;
- trust sources: identity, cases, personality, visible results, or information advantage;
- lifecycle: launch, growth, stable accumulation, or commercialization, with evidence limits;
- topic, format, visual-language, proof and CTA portfolios;
- high versus median versus mean-near versus low comparison on the same dimensions;
- publishing rhythm, eras, and content evolution;
- public comment demand and reply patterns with sample scope;
- observed recurring content system;
- products, services, sponsorships or other commercial paths that are publicly evidenced;
- conflicts, confounds, unavailable backend metrics, and other unknowns.

## Evidence rules

Each consequential statement must declare fact class, confidence, evidence references, applicable scope, and caveat. A creator-wide mechanism requires both portfolio-level recurrence and validated deep-video support. Likes alone cannot establish why content performed.

Do not produce copying advice, our positioning, a next-post proposal, rewritten titles, scripts, CTAs, or experiments. Those belong to `$content-strategy-workbench`.

Produce `creator-analysis.json`. If dates, comments, commercial evidence, or tier coverage are incomplete, preserve a typed partial section with the missing producer stage. Return `SYNTHESIS_READY` only when all present claims are supported; readiness does not require pretending unavailable evidence exists.
