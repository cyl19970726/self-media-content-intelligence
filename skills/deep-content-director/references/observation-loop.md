# Observation learning loop

Use this for 拉片, creator analysis, multi-post research and postmortems. The goal is transferable decisions, not admiration or a list of hooks.

## O1 Observe

Record what is actually said and shown:

- audience/problem/claim;
- opening and closing contracts;
- meaning changes and relations;
- proof objects and carrier conflicts;
- visual, text and audio functions;
- public metrics with capture time and limitations;
- missing channels and unknowns.

Separate the video's taught method from the method used by the video itself.

## O2 Model

Map available nodes without filling absent ones:

`person → need → positioning → persona → topic → script → proof → image → packaging → distribution → behavior → review`

Represent prerequisites, exceptions, alternatives and contradictions. A list is not a process until dependencies are explicit.

## O3 Ask back

For every proposed lesson ask:

1. Who and what problem does it serve?
2. What is evidence versus creator authority?
3. Which stage, resources, identity and platform conditions are required?
4. What counterexample would overturn it?
5. Does another source conflict?
6. What specific production action follows?
7. What observable outcome would support or limit it?

## O4 Contrast

Across sources mark patterns as:

- `single_observation`;
- `repeated_pattern`;
- `contradicted`;
- `experiment_supported`;
- `experiment_limited`.

Distinguish high performance from useful method. Lower-performing or non-tutorial posts can reveal identity, tone, proof, portfolio role and failure boundaries.

## O5 Apply

Create an original reconstruction for the user's account. Change at least audience, promise, proof or situation; normally change two. Specify what was learned, what was deliberately not copied and what new hypothesis is introduced.

Turn analysis into a brief, script, shot plan, edit decisions and publication experiment. If none is produced, the analysis is incomplete.

## O6 Review and update

After publication, compare prediction with evidence. Confirm, limit, contradict or leave the rule unresolved. Never promote a pattern because it appears in several posts by the same creator; shared persona, audience and production are confounds.

For method development, freeze a version before reading holdouts. Record a hash and sample split. Evaluate on unseen formats and topics, then write a separate delta report rather than rewriting history.
