# Xiaohongshu publishing

Platform behavior changes. Verify current official policies when a plan depends on them; otherwise treat these as design heuristics, not algorithm facts.

## Two discovery intents

### Search / deliberate use

- express the user's problem in natural query language;
- make title, cover, opening and body answer the same question;
- front-load prerequisites and scope;
- create a structure worth returning to: checklist, steps, comparison, template, failure modes;
- place keywords naturally in title/body rather than stuffing tags;
- track search exposure and later-window saves/target actions separately from first-day feed data.

### Feed / browsing

- create low-friction relevance, tension, identification, surprise or visible proof in the first frame;
- maintain narrative/cognitive movement;
- do not hide prerequisites until late;
- preserve an account-fit follow reason, not only a one-off spectacle.

A post can serve both, but state the primary intent.

## Packaging contract

- Title: specific person/problem/decision + distinct outcome or tension. Avoid unsupported superlatives and guaranteed results.
- Cover: one hierarchy, legible focal phrase, complementary proof/image. Do not repeat a long title verbatim.
- First 3 seconds: orient audience and deliver proof, consequence or unresolved tension. Skip generic self-introduction unless identity itself is necessary proof.
- Body: repay the exact promise; include conditions, examples and failure modes.
- Ending: close the opening, give one action, then a truthful follow/series reason.

## Content roles and metrics

Match evaluation to the pre-registered role:

- acquisition: qualified reach, 3-second/first-screen hold, target-audience comment share;
- follow: effective-view-to-follow, profile visit to follow/target action;
- search/save: search exposure, save rate, later-window reach, return/target action when available;
- trust/proof: qualified questions, profile depth, consultation or next-step quality;
- commercial: qualified click/inquiry/conversion and guardrail trust/retention;
- expression/taste: intended audience resonance and downstream account behavior, not a post-hoc excuse for poor reach.

Likes are surface feedback, never a universal success metric. Public save counts are not rewatch data.

## Comment and series operations

Use comments to audit whether the intended point landed, find objections and source candidate topics. Separate repeated need from a loud minority. Do not expose private messages or user identities without consent.

For series, maintain a visible directory, completed/next status, entry episode, and each episode's new value. Track movement from episode to profile/series and next-episode intent, not just per-post likes.

## Experiment cautions

Xiaohongshu post variants are usually not randomized. Time, initial audience, cover/title, distribution, trends and account state confound results. Prefer rotating comparable variants, recording natural exposure, changing one primary variable and requiring repeated directional evidence before promoting a rule.

Do not use risky automation, engagement manipulation, fake accounts, fake screenshots, unlicensed media or misleading health/business claims.
