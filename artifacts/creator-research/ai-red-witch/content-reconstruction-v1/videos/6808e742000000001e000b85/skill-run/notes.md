# Reconstruction repair notes

## Evidence resampling

The first targeted capture used the Skill's default 360×640 frames. That was sufficient for burned captions and large headings but insufficient for the small project-page prose. The project-page interval was therefore resampled directly from the supplied video at its original 1440×2560 resolution:

- `RESAMPLED-0001` — 2.2 s
- `RESAMPLED-0002` — 4.8 s
- `RESAMPLED-0003` — 6.4 s

The frames and original resampling manifest remain under `targeted-evidence/resampled/` and `targeted-evidence/resampled-evidence.json`. A separate Vision OCR pass remains at `targeted-evidence/resampled-ocr-evidence.json`; both artifacts stay registered in `reconstruction.derivedSources` for provenance. For canonical CLI resolution, `RESAMPLED-0001`–`RESAMPLED-0003` are also registered directly in the primary `targeted-evidence.json`, and the visually accepted OCR readings are merged into the primary `ocr-evidence.json` under unique IDs `OCR-00297`–`OCR-00301`. Reconstruction KU/REL/CQ references use those primary IDs; the derived artifacts preserve how the high-resolution evidence was produced.

## Full-track audio semantic inspection

The complete 87.96-second audio track was extracted to `audio/full-track-16k.wav` and inspected in overlapping 10-second windows with a 5-second hop using the MIT Audio Spectrogram Transformer fine-tuned on AudioSet. The resulting `audio/audio-semantic-evidence.json` is registered as `SRC-AUDIO-NON-SPEECH` (`audio.non_speech`). All 17 windows rank `Speech` and `Music` as their top two labels; Music scores range from approximately 0.366 to 0.937. This supports continuous background music beneath narration, functioning as a pacing/emotional bed rather than carrying factual content. Low, unstable labels such as Plop, Spray, and Zipper are not accepted as specific sound-effect events. Track title, creator, source, license, precise onset/offset, and possible embedded-sample audio remain unknown.

## Boundary model

These propositions are intentionally separate:

1. The project page visibly states that weights and inference code are provided.
2. The presenter claims the product is free and open source and has an online no-deployment experience.
3. The page visibly describes its demos as 30-second videos generated by the SkyReels-V2 diffusion-forcing model.
4. The social video itself exposes about 15.92 seconds across makeup, grassland, and turtle samples; the longest clearly verifiable same-scene window is about 8.8 seconds.
5. The project title uses `无限长胶片生成模型`; a later technical-page figure uses the qualified `几乎无限长度`; narration escalates to absolute `无限时长` and `持续无限延长`.

The page statements are stronger internal evidence than narration alone, but they do not externally verify license terms, zero price, current download availability, a full 30-second uncut playback, or literal infinite generation.

## Method note

`coverageMatrix.coreEvidence` counts whether the reconstruction's own core units carry resolvable evidence. It is not an independent completeness verdict. No independent evaluation, gate report, or readiness label was produced or modified in this repair.
