## Cognitive change

## Meaning changes and relations

The main chain is:

`authority hook` → `universal learning promise` → `claimed traffic-timing change` → `value/long-tail interpretation` → `collection priority` → `two creation branches` → `Bilibili-like medium/long closing payoff`.

This is edited argumentative order. It does not prove the causal links. The opening collage rhetorically supports authority, and the booklet rhetorically supports the policy discussion, but neither validates the claims.

Four decision dependencies are missing:

1. Attributable policy source, effective date, scope, and metric definition.
2. Analytics establishing collection as the top-ranked signal.
3. Resolved support-program identity, access, eligibility, benefits, platform, and region.
4. Input/condition/result evidence establishing that the formats caused high traffic.
