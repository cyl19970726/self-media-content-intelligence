# Independent audit — `6a3632b4000000000803f2c5`

This is a raw-source audit, not an evaluation of any candidate. I used only the original MP4/SRT and this video's evidence pack. Candidate runs, prior reports, study plans, and other-video outputs were excluded.

## Cognitive change

The video first blocks the expectation of a normal tutorial (“today there is no dry content”), then announces a 30-day free course and turns that promise into a populist mission: self-media should return opportunity to ordinary people, courses/materials should not become a knowledge monopoly, and charging may instead target capitalized creators and enterprises.

This is a pledge and values argument. It is not evidence that the course was later delivered, that learners reached 100k followers, or that every no-fee anecdote occurred as described.

## Meaning changes and relations

The edited chain is:

`profile/metrics authority` → `tutorial withheld` → `30-day free-course announcement` → `alleged monetization conflict` → `rights/anti-monopoly mission` → `no-paid-course/material pledge` → `beneficiary/brand reframe` → `anecdotal inserts` → `customer-class charging boundary` → `桃李满天下 payoff`.

Important relationship limits:

- The comments are juxtaposed as rebuttal but do not verify the company event.
- The profile's service offerings are scope-distinct from the narrower course/material pledge; treating them as either identical or wholly unrelated would be wrong.
- The chat/transaction inserts are offered as support for the anecdotes, but their referents and causal links remain unknown.
- Closing symbolism pays off the promise semantically, not operationally.

Five required dependencies are missing: dated growth baseline/ownership; course access/publication/learner outcomes; attributable company conversation; resolved job/transaction/fee chain; and published scope/price/classification rules for free versus paid offerings.
