# Creator Analysis OS V1 — Research Methodology Contract

Status: **confirmed core method**

Executable companion contracts: [three-lens-video-contract.md](three-lens-video-contract.md), [creator-depth-parity.md](creator-depth-parity.md), and [research-learning-model.md](research-learning-model.md). They define the schema fields, minimum evidence, count assertions, revision rules, and hard gates required to implement this methodology.

## 1. Why this product exists

The product is not a report generator and not a library of attractive cards. Its purpose is to help the operator build an increasingly accurate mental model of content:

- what a video actually communicates;
- how a director makes that communication understandable and persuasive;
- how image, interface, performance, sound, and editing carry or weaken meaning;
- how those choices vary across a creator's baseline, breakouts, and failures;
- which observations remain stable when more creators and more videos are added.

The unit of progress is not “one more page rendered.” It is one more evidence-backed distinction the user can make.

## 2. The three analytical lenses

Every deep video analysis uses three connected lenses. None may be replaced by the other two.

### 2.1 Content restoration — what is communicated

Restore the video so that a reader can recover its important information without watching it end to end:

- thesis and intended viewer change;
- complete knowledge units, steps, claims, examples, counterexamples, conditions, limitations, and CTA;
- readable article plus verbatim transcript;
- procedure dependencies for tutorials;
- claim–evidence–condition–counterexample–action relationships for argument or strategy content;
- explicit separation of raw fact, visual observation, author claim, system inference, conflict, and unknown.

Success is not a short summary. Success means removing the video would not remove a key idea from the restored article, while every consequential statement can still be traced to timecoded evidence.

### 2.2 Directing logic — how the communication is designed

Explain the sequence of cognitive and emotional decisions made for the viewer:

- the first question or desire activated;
- the promise made and when it becomes concrete;
- the order in which context, proof, examples, tension, and payoff are revealed;
- the information withheld, compressed, repeated, or delayed;
- transitions between cognitive states;
- proof grammar and credibility construction;
- comprehension cost, information load, pacing, and payoff distance;
- how the ending resolves, reframes, or leaves open the initial promise.

This is not a forced hook/body/CTA template. The first probe must discover the actual meaning changes and relationships before assigning a structure.

### 2.3 Visual and editing logic — how meaning is carried audiovisually

Explain what the viewer sees and hears, why it appears at that moment, and what evidentiary role it performs:

- orientation, composition, presenter position, screen/UI, examples, assets, captions, and text density;
- shot boundaries, meaningful visual changes, shot duration, cut density, montage, before/after, and result timing;
- cue ↔ representative frame ↔ all overlapping shots;
- sparse and dense views;
- operation before/during/after states for procedural content;
- OCR, interface state, inputs, parameters, outputs, and visible failure/success signatures;
- non-speech audio, emphasis, pauses, and transition roles where auditable;
- whether the image proves, explains, demonstrates, decorates, distracts, or contradicts the narration.

Montage adjacency never proves tool causality, execution continuity, product capability, authorization, or commercial rights.

## 3. From video understanding to creator understanding

A creator analysis has two evidence depths:

1. **Portfolio depth:** every visible post is used to establish the quantitative baseline, topic/format system, publishing rhythm, eras, and candidate performance groups.
2. **Reconstruction depth:** a bounded, representative set is analyzed through all three lenses above.

The creator is interpreted through variance, not only through winners:

```text
full corpus
  → distribution and data health
  → High / Base / Low comparison
  → 21 canonical comparison records
  → High / median-near / arithmetic-mean-near / Low × 3 deep videos
  → media-type verification and unique reconstruction set (normally 12; overlaps deduplicated)
  → positioning, values, content system, breakout mechanisms, failure mechanisms
```

### High / Base / Low reasoning

- **High** identifies multiple ways content breaks above the creator's own baseline.
- **Base** establishes what the creator normally produces and what normally works; it must include median-near, mean-near, and typical-form anchors.
- **Low** reveals delayed value, weak proof, format mismatch, excessive load, poor packaging, audience mismatch, or other failure conditions without equating low public likes with worthless content.

The portfolio tiers and deep groups are related but not identical. Detail enrichment must confirm media type before the final deep set is frozen. Image/text posts remain valid comparison evidence but cannot satisfy a deep-video slot. Median-near and arithmetic-mean-near each require three candidates unless the same post genuinely belongs to both groups; an overlap is recorded, not assumed.

The system must compare the same dimensions across tiers: user problem, promise, viewer change, proof, directing sequence, audiovisual form, comprehension cost, public signals, era, and confounds.

## 4. How creator conclusions are formed

Positioning is an output of evidence, not profile-copy paraphrase. It is derived from:

- repeated user problems and values across the full portfolio;
- recurring content lanes and proof modes;
- the role of identity and personality;
- what High amplifies, what Base stabilizes, and what Low fails to deliver;
- visible products, services, partnerships, and business behavior;
- longitudinal changes in topic, form, production quality, and audience promise.

A creator conclusion must therefore answer:

- who the creator is and whom the account serves;
- what practical, cognitive, emotional, social, decision, or trust value it repeatedly provides;
- what constitutes the account's stable baseline;
- which distinct breakout mechanisms exist;
- which failure mechanisms exist;
- what is stable, conditional, anomalous, contradicted, or still unknown.

Research does not yet prescribe what our own account should copy or publish.

## 5. The learning loop

The methodology is designed to improve through real analyses rather than freeze the first taxonomy.

After every completed creator:

1. record newly observed information carriers, directing devices, proof modes, visual grammars, and failure modes;
2. test whether they already fit the open data model;
3. add a new label or relationship only when existing concepts cannot represent the evidence;
4. re-evaluate prior cross-creator conclusions when a new creator supplies confirming or contradicting evidence;
5. promote a pattern from video-specific → creator-specific → conditional → track-wide only when its evidence scope justifies the change.

The workbench must preserve this learning as versioned research infrastructure: schemas, open taxonomies, selection rules, evaluation cases, and reusable evidence queries—not as hidden prompt intuition.

## 6. Depth gates

A creator page is not considered analytically complete merely because it renders.

It must pass all of the following:

- corpus coverage and collection failures are visible;
- baseline statistics reproduce from portfolio data or disclose that only aggregates survive;
- topic and format clusters show count, share, median, mean, maximum, high count/share, and annotation basis when available;
- High/Base/Low use creator-relative rules and expose denominator and confounds;
- List and Gallery project the same record IDs;
- every comparison record retains core content, architecture, mechanism, metrics, and evidence state;
- all deep-selected videos expose the three analytical lenses;
- consequential video conclusions link to transcript/frame/shot/OCR/audio evidence as applicable;
- an unfinished or failed reconstruction is never presented as validated;
- boilerplate, placeholders, or legacy-adapter omissions cannot satisfy parity tests merely by conforming to a schema.

## 7. Reference standard

The existing AI红发魔女 research remains the minimum depth reference because it demonstrates:

- a full-corpus baseline distinct from the 21-record comparison set;
- separate topic and format portfolios;
- High, median/base, and Low mechanism analysis;
- multiple breakout mechanisms rather than one generic success explanation;
- real per-video content architecture, transcript, sparse/dense frames, and evidence boundaries.

Future unified pages may improve its information architecture, but must not contain less analytical information than the source artifacts unless a field is explicitly marked unavailable with its reason.
